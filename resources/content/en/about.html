GEOFLORESTA is a Mozambican consultancy in geotechnology, forestry and the environment, based in the City of Beira, Sofala province. It was founded in 2026.

What we do

We work with geographic data in four areas: Geographic Information Systems, remote sensing, artificial intelligence applied to spatial data, and environmental consultancy. Besides client projects, we publish on this site open data on Mozambique prepared from openly licensed sources, monitoring maps, and free tutorials for R, Google Colab, Google Earth Engine and QGIS.

Founder

Guilherme Francisco Fernando Chale is a Forest Engineer, a graduate of Lúrio University, Faculty of Agricultural Sciences, in Niassa, northern Mozambique. He works on the sustainable management of natural resources and supports environmental projects.

“I believe it is possible to grow economically without compromising the future of our planet. GEOFLORESTA is my contribution to a greener and more sustainable Mozambique.”

— Guilherme Francisco Fernando Chale

How we work

  1. Assessment — analysis of the situation and environmental context of the area.
  2. Planning — definition of the method, data and deliverables.
  3. Delivery — processing, analysis and map production, with checks on the results.
  4. Follow-up — support after delivery and evaluation of the results.

Our principles are sustainability, technical rigour and transparency: every map and dataset we publish states its source, licence and date.
