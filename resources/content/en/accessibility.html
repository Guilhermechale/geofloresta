GEOFLORESTA wants the geofloresta.com website to be usable by everyone, including people using a screen reader, keyboard only, magnification or high contrast.

Compliance status

Our target is level AA of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2. The site is partially compliant: it meets most AA criteria, with the exceptions listed below.

What we have done

  • Full keyboard navigation, with a visible focus indicator and a skip-to-content link.
  • Colour contrast checked in both light and dark themes.
  • Heading structure, landmarks and labels for screen readers; the language of every page is declared.
  • The system’s reduced-motion preference is respected.
  • Alternative text on images; text can be enlarged up to 200% without loss of content.
  • The cookie banner and panel work with the keyboard and the Esc key.

Known limitations

  • Interactive maps are drawn by the browser and cannot be read by screen readers. The same information is available as tables and downloadable files (CSV, GeoJSON).
  • Some older documents migrated from the previous site may have images without a full description. We are fixing them.

Report a problem

If you meet a barrier, write to {{contact_email}} with the page address and a description. We reply within 10 working days and, if we cannot fix it straight away, we send you the information in another format.

How it was assessed

Self-assessment with automated tools (Lighthouse and contrast checks) and manual keyboard testing, in light and dark themes and on screens from 390 to 1920 pixels.
