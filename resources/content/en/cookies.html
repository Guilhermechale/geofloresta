Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser. Local storage (localStorage) is a similar technology. This page lists everything the geofloresta.com website stores on your device.

1. What we use

We only use what the site needs to work and, with your permission, what remembers your language and theme preferences. We use no advertising and no social media cookies. {{analytics_status}}

No non-essential cookie or script is loaded before you give consent. If you refuse, the site works exactly the same.

3. Changing or withdrawing consent

Use the “Cookie preferences” link in the footer of any page. Withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it. Your choice is valid for 6 months; after that we ask again. If this policy changes, the notice appears again.

You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block necessary cookies, the contact form will stop working.

4. Requests to other servers

The maps request images from OpenFreeMap and NASA GIBS. These requests set no cookies, but your IP address reaches those servers, as with any request on the Internet. See the Privacy Policy.
