This policy explains what personal data the geofloresta.com website processes, why, for how long, and what your rights are. It applies to anyone who visits the site, downloads data or sends us a message.

1. Controller

GEOFLORESTA — City of Beira, Sofala, Mozambique. Privacy contact: {{contact_email}}.

2. What data we process and why

2.1 Contact form

Data: name, email, organisation (optional), subject, message, page language and date sent. We also store a cryptographic fingerprint of the IP address (a keyed hash that cannot be reversed to the IP) to limit abusive submissions.

Purpose: to answer your request and, where relevant, prepare a proposal. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in replying to people who contact us (Article 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR, for visitors in the European Union).

Retention: 24 months from submission; the message is then deleted automatically, unless it leads to a contract, in which case the legal retention periods for business records apply.

2.2 Data downloads

We count how many times each file is downloaded. The count is anonymous: we store no IP, cookie or identifier of the person downloading. No registration is required.

2.3 Cookie consent record

When you choose cookie categories we store a random identifier, the text version, the categories chosen, the language and the date, so that we can demonstrate consent. We do not store the IP. Retention: 24 months.

2.4 Server logs

Our host (Hostinger) automatically logs, like any web server, the IP address, date and time, requested page and browser type, for security and troubleshooting. These logs are managed by Hostinger and kept for the period it sets.

2.6 Maps

When you open the maps, your browser requests images directly from the OpenFreeMap (basemap) and NASA GIBS (satellite imagery) servers. As with any request on the Internet, those services receive your IP address. We send them no other data and they set no cookies through our site.

3. Recipients

  • Hostinger — website hosting, database and domain mailbox.
  • Google — only if you accept the “Analytics” category and only while Google Analytics is active.
  • OpenFreeMap and NASA — receive the IP when the browser requests map images.
  • Authorities — only where required by law.

We do not sell or share personal data for commercial purposes.

4. International transfers

Hostinger and Google servers may be located outside Mozambique and the European Union. In that case the contractual safeguards of those providers apply.

5. Your rights

You can ask for access to your data, its correction or erasure, object to processing, request restriction or portability, and withdraw consent at any time (without affecting processing already carried out). To withdraw cookie consent, use “Cookie preferences” in the footer.

To exercise these rights, write to {{contact_email}}. We reply within 30 days at most. We may ask for reasonable information to confirm your identity. If you are in the European Union, you may also complain to your national data protection authority.

6. Security

The site uses HTTPS on every page. The admin area requires a strong password and two-factor authentication. Messages are kept in a database with no public access, and we make regular backups.

7. Children

The site is not intended to collect data from children. If you know a minor has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Legal framework

We process data in accordance with the Mozambican legislation applicable to personal data protection and electronic transactions and, for visitors in the European Union, Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page with the date of update.
